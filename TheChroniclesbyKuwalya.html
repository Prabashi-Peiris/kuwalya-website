නිරාකාර රූප The Art of Draping

නිරාකාර රූප is a storytelling form of draping where each creation moves beyond structure and becomes a medium of expression. Through fluid design, texture, and movement, these pieces carry messages of strength, positivity, and quiet transformation.

This draping series invites viewers to experience garments not only as clothing but as narrative art. Each fold, pleat, and drape embodies emotion, intention, and personal storytelling, reflecting the journey of creativity and human expression.
