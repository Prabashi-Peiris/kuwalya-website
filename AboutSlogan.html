Our slogan, “The Glory of Hundred Petals,” is inspired by Kuwalya itself another name for the lotus. Just as the lotus blooms with grace and strength, representing purity, resilience, and beauty, so too does Sri Lanka shine through its culture, heritage, and artistry. Through this symbolism, Kuwalya celebrates the glory of our roots our traditions, our craftsmanship, and our people while sharing it with the world. Every creation is a petal of this story, crafted to uplift, inspire, and spread positivity, reflecting the full beauty and potential of Sri Lankan design.
